# That Roofer Chick: Elevated

Build a premium, highly creative website for “That Roofer Chick”, a personal roofing brand based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.

BUSINESS INFO
Name: That Roofer Chick
Tagline: “Climbing the ladder 1 rung at a time! From labourer to contractor. 14yrs goes quick!”
Location: Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
From: Calabogie, Ontario
Reviews: 100% recommend (6 Reviews)
Phone: +1 613-801-8582
TikTok: @thatrooferchick
Instagram: @thatrooferchick

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT create a normal roofing website. Make this feel like an expensive personal brand, editorial magazine and premium roofing portfolio combined.

DESIGN:
Use a warm ivory base with terracotta, burnt orange, muted sage green, soft beige and charcoal accents. Do NOT use a single-color design and do NOT make the website fully dark.

HERO:
Create an unusual cinematic hero — full-screen rooftop/work video with layered typography, an offset portrait/image card, subtle grain and an elegant “14 YEARS ON THE ROOF” badge.
Headline:
“14 Years. One Rung at a Time.”
Subtext:
“From labourer to contractor — built from the ground up.”
Add premium CTA buttons: “Work With Me” and “Explore My Journey”.

SECTIONS:
1. Premium floating navbar
2. Cinematic hero with video + image layering
3. 14-year personal journey timeline from labourer to contractor
4. Roofing services with large editorial image cards
5. Full-width cinematic roofing video section
6. “Behind The Roof” section with image + story
7. Before/After roofing project gallery
8. A dedicated video-only section showing real roofing work
9. Personal brand / “Why That Roofer Chick?” section
10. Mixed masonry gallery with photos and short videos
11. Customer reviews
12. Social media section for TikTok + Instagram
13. Large final CTA: “Ready to Get Your Roof Done Right?”
14. Premium footer

VISUAL EXPERIENCE:
Mix full-screen videos, image galleries, asymmetrical layouts, oversized typography, floating cards and generous whitespace. Some sections should be video-only and some image-only.

Use cinematic rooftop/work videos as background placeholders and roofing-related images where needed. Videos must autoplay, muted, loop and lazy-load.

Add smooth scrolling, subtle parallax, elegant reveal animations, magnetic-style buttons, image hover effects and polished page transitions. Keep animations smooth and lightweight.

Make the website fully responsive and fast.

MOST IMPORTANT:
The website must NOT look like a generic roofing contractor template.
It should feel like a $10,000+ premium personal-brand website — confident, memorable, editorial, cinematic and visually unique.

Use different visual compositions between sections so the page never feels repetitive or boring.
